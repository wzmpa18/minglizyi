3D acupoint layout data
========================

The schematic point coordinates used to derive acupoints-3d.json are adapted
from the Let Energy Flow dataset by the Let Energy Flow authors:

https://github.com/MuzikPro/letenergyflow

Dataset licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made by the Yan Dao Guoxue project:
- excluded the non-canonical GV29 entry to match the 361-point catalogue;
- normalised DU/GV, RN/CV, SJ/TE and LV/LR aliases;
- projected the source atlas coordinates onto the Z-Anatomy skin mesh;
- mirrored bilateral markers and attached the existing simplified-Chinese names.

acupoints-3d.json remains available under CC BY-SA 4.0. Every record retains
its source IDs and review status. The coordinates are schematic_unvalidated:
they are educational layout positions, not clinical measurements or guidance
for locating or needling a point on a real person.
